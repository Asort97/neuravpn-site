# Interface Assets

The native ripple interaction in `assets/js/ui-motion.js` adapts Magic UI's
Ripple Button. Dialog entry uses its Blur Fade pattern with blur disabled and
shorter timing. React, Tailwind and Motion are not bundled into this vanilla site.

Source: https://github.com/magicuidesign/magicui
